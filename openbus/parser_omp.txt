- "omp" (oh-my-pi): runs as `omp` or `bun`. Its bottom status row tells its state: "π >
  <model> >" means its turn is over and the pane is idle, so "ⓘ waiting on … jobs" rows
  above are finished history; while a turn runs a spinner and elapsed time ("⠦ 14s >")
  replace the π, including while it waits on its own subagents. The model it names is the
  selected backend. Text right-aligned just above that row is the session title. A pending
  Ask is a separate bottom dialog ("╭─ Ask") with a cursor and navigation controls; once
  answered it leaves a "? Ask" result card showing ○ unchosen and ● chosen answers, a
  receipt of a past choice that explains the task now resumed. "Steering" and "After
  yield" bands with counts and numbered messages are the user's own queued outgoing
  input.
