- "omp" (oh-my-pi): runs as `omp` or `bun`. Its bottom status row is decisive. Starting
  "π > <model> > 🌳 <repo>" it means the turn is over: idle, and any "ⓘ waiting on … jobs"
  rows above are finished history. While a turn is active a spinner and elapsed time
  ("⠦ 14s >") replace the π: running, including while it waits on its own subagents.
  Like OpenCode, the model it names is only the selected backend.
